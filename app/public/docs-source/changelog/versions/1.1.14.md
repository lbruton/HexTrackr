# Version 1.1.14 - Dialog and Error Text

**Release Status**: Released
**Release Date**: 2026-10-22
**Parent Issues**: HXTR-12

## Overview

Confirm dialogs name the action and its effect, and error messages say what failed and what to do next. Stale task notes and issue-ID prefixes are gone from the vulnerability routes and the backup service.

---

## [1.1.14]

### Changed

- Confirm dialogs for deleting Cisco PSIRT credentials, cancelling an operation, resetting a template and removing attached documentation name the action and its effect.
- Sign-in errors say how long to wait after a lockout (15 minutes) or rate limit (one minute).
- Errors in the audit log viewer, settings, ticket views and the progress modal say what to do next.

### Removed

- The task-note block at the end of `app/routes/vulnerabilities.js`.
- Issue-ID and `CRITICAL:` prefixes from comments in `app/services/backupService.js`.
